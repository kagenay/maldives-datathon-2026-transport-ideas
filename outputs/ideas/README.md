# Maldives Datathon 2026 transport ideas

Research reviewed 24 September 2026. The [official theme and FAQ](https://www.datathon.stats.gov.mv/faq) are broad, but the FAQ is more specific than the honours-page excerpt: it seeks better **collection, integration, analysis and use of transport data**, especially for Greater Malé; it explicitly names road, marine and pedestrian movement, inter-island connectivity, GIS dashboards, planning and resource allocation. A route map should therefore answer a measurable decision question, not only display lines. The event is set for 24 October 2026; the FAQ lists a 7-minute pitch and judging on relevance, data use, innovation, execution, clarity, impact and teamwork.

## Geography and bridge correction

Greater Malé is primarily a **road, bus and walking** planning case: the [Sinamalé Bridge](https://presidency.gov.mv/Press/Article/19547) already links Malé to the airport and Hulhumalé. The [President's Office reported](https://presidency.gov.mv/Press/Article/37257) that a Malé–Vilimalé span had been physically joined in August 2026 as part of the planned Malé–Vilimalé–Gulhifalhu–Thilafushi connection. **Public traffic opening and date are not verified.** Bridge-opening impacts in these briefs are scenarios, never observations. [MTCC's 2023 report](https://mtcc.mv/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Annual-Report-2023-2.pdf) listed Greater Malé ferry links to Vilimalé, Thilafushi, Gulhifalhu and Hulhumalé; their exact current operating status requires a fresh operator check, so we do not claim that only two routes exist. Ferry-centered ideas below now target outlying atolls or national connectivity, where sea travel remains central.

## Best five to investigate first

1. **[Thilamalé Bridge Mode Shift Lab](thilamale-bridge-mode-shift-lab.md)** — timely Greater Malé question with a real baseline and explicit future scenarios.
2. **[Street Safety Intervention Map](street-safety-intervention-map.md)** — collect a small real crossing pilot and make a clear street-design decision.
3. **[Accessible Journey Chain Audit](accessible-journey-chain-audit.md)** — show which sidewalk, crossing or bus-stop repair completes a journey.
4. **[Greater Malé Traffic Count Map](greater-male-traffic-count-map.md)** — a repeatable baseline for congestion and bridge approaches.
5. **[Greater Malé Bus Priority Tester](greater-male-bus-priority-tester.md)** — compare person-time savings from one reversible road-space change.

**Promising sixth:** [Ferry–Flight–Bus Connection Gap Map](ferry-flight-bus-connection-gap-map.md) develops the shared three-mode-map suggestion into a testable scheduling question. [RTL](https://www.rtl.mv/) shows ferry/bus information, [Maldivian](https://maldivian.aero/assets/files/Annual%20Report%202024.pdf) publishes a domestic network, and [Velana](https://velana.macl.aero/) displays flights. These public displays are not proof of reusable open feeds. [MACL's terms](https://macl.aero/terms-of-service) claim rights over flight feeds and restrict automated extraction; until a permitted feed is obtained, flight timings in the demo must be clearly synthetic.

## Numbered idea list

1. [Thilamalé Bridge Mode Shift Lab](thilamale-bridge-mode-shift-lab.md) — Compare plausible bus, motorcycle and walking access after the planned Malé–Vilimalé–Gulhifalhu–Thilafushi road link opens.
2. [Street Safety Intervention Map](street-safety-intervention-map.md) — Prioritize Greater Malé junctions by exposure, observed conflicts and feasible street changes.
3. [Accessible Journey Chain Audit](accessible-journey-chain-audit.md) — Identify where a nominally accessible Greater Malé bus journey fails between home, crossing, stop, vehicle and clinic.
4. [Greater Malé Traffic Count Map](greater-male-traffic-count-map.md) — Create repeatable junction-level vehicle counts to target congestion measures instead of relying on anecdote.
5. [Greater Malé Bus Priority Tester](greater-male-bus-priority-tester.md) — Test where a short bus-priority measure could save more traveler time than adding another vehicle to congestion.
6. [Ferry–Flight–Bus Connection Gap Map](ferry-flight-bus-connection-gap-map.md) — Put domestic air, RTL ferries and buses on one map to reveal where a traveler must wait overnight or cannot make a feasible transfer.
7. [Bridge Approach Bottleneck Map](bridge-approach-bottleneck-map.md) — Identify which junctions and crossings around the existing and planned Greater Malé bridges could become bottlenecks.
8. [Greater Malé Bus Crowding Pulse](greater-male-bus-crowding-pulse.md) — Measure where bus-stop queues and full vehicles make scheduled Greater Malé service less useful than it appears on a route map.
9. [Single-Link Failure Atlas](single-link-failure-atlas.md) — Identify which existing bridge, key road or atoll ferry link would disrupt the most essential journeys if closed.
10. [Rain-Flood Walking Detour Atlas](rain-flood-walking-detour-atlas.md) — Show how localized flooding changes safe walking access to bus stops, schools and clinics in Greater Malé.
11. [Bridge–Bus Interchange Walk Clock](bridge-bus-interchange-walk-clock.md) — Measure whether people can safely walk between bridge-side bus stops, crossings and key destinations within the available transfer time.
12. [Safe School Arrival Streets](safe-school-arrival-streets.md) — Rank school frontage crossings for safer walking and traffic management at drop-off time.
13. [Heat-and-Rain Walking Comfort Map](heat-and-rain-walking-comfort-map.md) — Locate exposed walks to bus stops, schools and clinics where shade or shelter would help the most people.
14. [Island-to-Clinic Access Atlas](island-to-clinic-access-atlas.md) — Map the actual scheduled time and transfer burden for each island to reach a higher-level health facility.
15. [Missed-Connection Ferry Heatmap](missed-connection-ferry-heatmap.md) — Find timetable transfers that are possible on paper but leave passengers with an unsafe or very long wait.
16. [After-Dark Island Access Gap](after-dark-island-access-gap.md) — Reveal which islands lose practical access to essential destinations after the last scheduled sailing.
17. [Weather-Resilient Sailing Windows](weather-resilient-sailing-windows.md) — Show which scheduled connections are exposed to official marine alerts and how alternate sailing windows could preserve access.
18. [Emergency Referral Travel Stress Test](emergency-referral-travel-stress-test.md) — Stress-test which islands lack a timely route to appropriate care when one sea or air link is unavailable.
19. [Island Fare Burden Lens](island-fare-burden-lens.md) — Compare the affordability of reaching essential services across islands and household types.
20. [Demand-Responsive Atoll Shuttle Testbed](demand-responsive-atoll-shuttle-testbed.md) — Test whether a small pre-booked speedboat service could fill gaps without replacing viable fixed routes.
21. [Port-to-Street Delivery Window Planner](port-to-street-delivery-window-planner.md) — Test how changing cargo unloading windows could reduce conflicts with peak walking and bus movement.
22. [Harbour Crossing Conflict Diary](harbour-crossing-conflict-diary.md) — Locate recurring conflicts among pedestrians, motorcycles, cargo carts and boarding queues near ferry terminals.
23. [Small-Ferry Fuel and Emissions Ledger](small-ferry-fuel-and-emissions-ledger.md) — Compare route-level fuel intensity and schedule changes for a lower-emission public ferry fleet.
24. [Essential Medicines Parcel Reach](essential-medicines-parcel-reach.md) — Test how ferry schedules affect the time to move essential medicine parcels to island clinics.
25. [Island Evacuation Pickup Planner](island-evacuation-pickup-planner.md) — Compare evacuation pickup sites and vessel allocation for isolated islands under a disaster scenario.

## Data and source cautions

- [MBS Census 2022 tables](https://statisticsmaldives.gov.mv/census-2022-results-summary/) give island population context, not trips. [MBS OneMap](https://statisticsmaldives.gov.mv/atoll-profiles/) already maps census topics; transport decisions need an additional layer and analysis.
- [RTL](https://www.rtl.mv/) already provides route maps, schedules, tracking and tickets. A license for bulk reuse or a national machine-readable feed was **not verified**. Do not claim to have created those existing functions.
- The [FAQ](https://www.datathon.stats.gov.mv/faq) says MBS will provide Census 2022/HIES 2019 and also prohibits private, nonpublic or IP-restricted data. Confirm terms of any event-provided files and operator data before building. Separate observed, public and synthetic values visibly.
- The exact active Greater Malé ferry inventory, future bridge traffic-opening date, and availability of rights-cleared domestic flight timetables remain **not verified**. Recheck them immediately before choosing a build.
