# Bridge–Bus Interchange Walk Clock

**Pitch.** Measure whether people can safely walk between bridge-side bus stops, crossings and key destinations within the available transfer time.

**Type:** Original

**Research status:** 24 September 2026; concept document. The planned western bridge is treated only as a future scenario.

## Problem, users and decision

A short map distance can hide long crossings, missing curb ramps or indirect bus-stop access. City planners, MTCC and accessibility advocates need measured transfer paths as Greater Malé gains another road link.

**Decision supported:** Should a bus stop, crossing phase or walking connection be changed to make a bridge-side transfer workable?

## Precedent and Maldivian equivalent

**Foreign precedent:** Original Maldives proposal; no foreign implementation claimed.

**Maldives evidence and confidence:** [RTL](https://www.rtl.mv/) publishes bus routes, while the [President's Office](https://presidency.gov.mv/Press/Article/37257) documents the future Malé–Vilimalé connection. A public audit of bridge-side walking transfers is **not verified**. Confidence: high on the components; low on product status.

## Prototype interaction

Map the walking leg at an existing Sinamalé Bridge-side interchange; a clock animates exit, crossing wait and walk to the next bus or destination. Toggle a curb ramp or crossing improvement and show the changed margin. A planned western-bridge stop can appear as a separately marked hypothetical design.

## Data plan

**Available now / collectable by the team:** Public [RTL](https://www.rtl.mv/) bus stop/schedule details, dated [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) footpaths, and repeated team walks at one safe current interchange: start/end point, crossing wait, distance, barriers, time and mobility profile where consented. Pilot: one Malé or Hulhumalé transfer; several one-day walks.

**Future data to collect and method:** MTCC publishes actual bus door-open/close timestamps by trip_id/stop_id. Road authority audits sidewalk width, ramps, signal phases and planned bridge stop locations with geotagged inspection forms; update after works and at least quarterly. No passenger tracking needed.

## Demo and rollout

**Datathon demo:** Compare observed pilot walk times with published bus departure margins; show one **hypothetical** stop/crossing change. Future bridge walking layouts remain scenario geometry until official plans are confirmed.

**Longer-term rollout:** Audit each bridge-side interchange, co-design improvements with disabled users, then measure actual transfer success after opening.

## Value and safeguards

**Expected benefit:** Makes road links useful to people who do not drive.

**Limits:** Healthy-adult walk times do not represent all users; route design may change.

**Privacy:** Time observer walks, not strangers; photos exclude faces and plates.

**Dependencies:** Safe field access, confirmed stop locations and accessibility review.

## Datathon fit and ratings

The [2026 theme](https://www.datathon.stats.gov.mv/faq) includes pedestrian movement and inclusive planning. A measured path and one improvement make a simple interactive pitch.

**Feasibility: High — a small real walking audit is possible now.**

**Distinctiveness: Medium-high — tests the last meters of bridge access rather than only vehicle travel time.**
