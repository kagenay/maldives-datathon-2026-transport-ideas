# Harbour Crossing Conflict Diary

**Pitch.** Locate recurring conflicts among pedestrians, motorcycles, cargo carts and boarding queues near ferry terminals.

**Type:** Original  
**Research status:** 23 September 2026; concept document. Comparable Maldivian implementation status is assessed below.

## Problem, users and decision

Harbour edges mix modes in a small space. Councils and terminal operators need safe access evidence.

**Decision supported:** Which terminal approach should be redesigned or time-separated first?

## Precedent and Maldivian equivalent

**Foreign precedent:** Original Maldives proposal; no foreign implementation claimed.

**Maldives evidence and confidence:** [MTCC](https://mtcc.mv/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Annual-Report-2023-2.pdf) reports terminal renovation in Greater Malé and [police](https://www.police.gov.mv/crime-statistics) report traffic-accident counts; a public harbour-access conflict diary is **not verified**. Confidence: medium-high on context; low on gap.

## Prototype interaction

Terminal access map with 15-minute counts by movement and conflict type; compare a cargo delivery window shift or a marked walking path.

## Data plan

**Available now / collectable by the team:** [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) streets/wharves, [RTL](https://www.rtl.mv/) scheduled calls, and a safely observed, anonymized pilot at one public terminal: location, time band, mode counts and near-miss category. Once for demo.

**Future data to collect and method:** Terminal managers perform standardized weekly conflict counts and record incident_id, location, time, modes, severity, cargo operation and corrective action; review monthly.

**Geographic coverage:** Pilot — one public terminal; later selected high-conflict terminals.

**Update frequency:** Census 2022 is a fixed release; where used, take a dated [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) snapshot. Recheck any public timetable, fare or alert immediately before presentation. Team observations are one-off pilot snapshots. The future collection cadence is specified above; no live feed is assumed for the demo.

Where a future input is required, any generated values in the datathon view must be marked **synthetic scenario data**, visually separated from observed/public data. The [official FAQ](https://www.datathon.stats.gov.mv/faq) allows public or newly gathered credited data, says MBS will provide Census 2022/HIES 2019 to participants, and also prohibits private/nonpublic sources; confirm the scope and terms of event-provided files before using them.

## Demo and rollout

**Datathon demo:** Show one real observation sheet if permitted; otherwise use conspicuously synthetic counts and an empty field protocol.

**Longer-term rollout:** Test reversible signs/barriers or delivery windows, repeating the same count method before and after.

## Value and safeguards

**Expected benefit:** Safer pedestrian access and smoother operations.

**Limits:** Near-miss coding is subjective and depends on observer position.

**Privacy:** No photos of people, faces, plates or named staff.

**Dependencies:** Terminal permission and safety protocol.

## Datathon fit and ratings

The [2026 theme and criteria](https://www.datathon.stats.gov.mv/faq) emphasize transport data systems, GIS, decision-making, relevance, data use, innovation, technical execution, clarity, impact and teamwork. **Fit:** Participatory mapping and an operational decision; narrow scope aids demo.

**Feasibility: Medium — access may be restricted.**  
**Distinctiveness: High — cross-mode harbour edge is locally salient.**
